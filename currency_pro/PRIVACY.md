# Privacy Policy — CurrencyPro

Public URL:
https://sites.google.com/view/mob-apps-inc/privacy-policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

CurrencyPro is a reference currency converter that runs on your device.

## What the app collects

CurrencyPro does **not** create user accounts and does **not** upload
favourites, alerts, trips, holdings or conversion history. Those stay in
on-device storage (`SharedPreferences`).

## Network requests

To show live prices the app requests public market data from:

- open.er-api.com (fiat exchange rates)
- frankfurter.dev / frankfurter.app (ECB reference rates and history)
- api.coingecko.com (Bitcoin, Ethereum, gold and silver proxies)
- cdn.jsdelivr.net (last-resort fiat table)

Requests include a generic User-Agent so the hosts can identify the client.

## Advertising

The app shows banner, native and full-screen ads from Google AdMob.
Google and its partners may collect a device advertising identifier and
data about ad views and clicks to serve and measure ads. You can opt out
of personalized ads in your device settings (Google settings › Ads on
Android, or Tracking on iOS).

## Permissions (Android)

- `INTERNET` and `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` — download rates and ads
- `VIBRATE` — optional keypad haptics (off if you disable it in Settings)
- `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` — optional rate-alert notifications (asked on onboarding)
- `AD_ID` — used by AdMob for advertising on Android

## Sharing

The Copy / Send buttons only share text you generate. Share uses the system
share sheet; CurrencyPro does not send that text to our servers.
